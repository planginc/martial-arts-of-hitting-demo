The Method

Why "martial arts" of hitting?

A great swing and a great strike come from the same place. Most lessons fix the bat. We start with the body, the breath, and the mind, so the swing holds up when the pitch is real.

01

Stance

A rooted, athletic base. Players learn to feel balance before they ever take a cut.

02

Load

Coiling power the way a fighter loads a strike: quiet, controlled, and on time.

03

Strike

Hips lead, hands follow. Short, fast, repeatable contact through the zone.

04

Focus

Breathing, patience, and confidence at the plate. The mental side most lessons skip.

The Belt Path

Every player knows where they stand.

Kids earn belts as they master each skill, just like a dojo. Parents see real milestones, and players get something to work toward every week.

White

Stance, grip, and balance fundamentals.

Yellow

Load and timing off the tee and soft toss.

Green

Consistent hard contact on live front toss.

Blue

Pitch recognition and all-fields hitting.

Black

Game-speed velocity, approach, and plate discipline.

Meet Your Coach (sample bio)

Coach Steve

Steve grew up in the batting cage. He played four years of varsity baseball, then two seasons of college ball, where he led his team in on-base percentage as a sophomore. Along the way he trained in karate, and he kept noticing the same thing: the best strikes and the best swings were built the same way.

After 12 years of coaching youth players, he put those two worlds together and created Martial Arts of Hitting. His goal is simple: give every kid a swing they trust and the confidence to use it.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

What ages do you coach?

Players from about age 7 through high school, baseball and softball.

Does my child need karate experience?

No. The martial arts piece is a way of training balance, timing, and focus. No kicks or sparring involved.

What should we bring?

A bat, a helmet, and batting gloves if they use them. Cage time is included.

Can we try a lesson before joining?

Yes. Most families start with a single $40 lesson, which includes a swing assessment and belt placement.
